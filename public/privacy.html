Your information

Privacy notice

This draft explains how POMAS intends to handle information submitted through its poultry farmer research survey. Participation is voluntary. The details below must be checked against the live Google Form and actual research practices before this notice is relied on.

In short: We use survey responses to understand poultry farm practices and inform POMAS research. The survey is hosted by Google Forms. Google acts as a processor by processing submitted information to provide that service and may also handle technical information under its own terms.

Who is responsible?

The POMAS research initiative is responsible for deciding why survey information is collected and how it is used for this research. For privacy requests, contact {{PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

What information may be collected?

The form may collect the answers you submit about poultry farming, farm operations and record-keeping. If you choose to provide contact details for a follow-up, those details may also be collected. Google Forms may process technical information needed to operate the service. Depending on the form settings, Google may also collect account or email information. Confirm the live form settings and list every required and optional field before publication.

Do not submit passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers or personal information that is not needed to answer the research questions.

Why is it used?

POMAS uses submitted information to understand how poultry farms manage flocks, feed, health, production, inventory, sales, expenses and records. Contact details supplied for a follow-up are used to arrange that follow-up. We do not sell survey responses.

Google Forms

Google provides Google Forms and processes submitted form information on POMAS's behalf to provide the service. Google may also process technical information under its own terms. POMAS does not control Google's independent processing. Read Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

How long is information kept?

The research team must set and follow a retention period that is no longer than necessary for the stated research and any requested follow-up. The actual retention period and deletion process must be confirmed before this notice is published. Do not rely on this draft until that schedule is documented.

Your rights under Nigeria's NDPA 2023

Subject to the conditions and limits in applicable data-protection law, you may have rights to request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, or to object to certain processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Some requests may not be possible after information has been irreversibly anonymised or where a legal exception applies.

Security and sharing

Access to research responses should be limited to people who need the information for this research. Google processes form data to provide Google Forms. The actual access controls, sharing settings, storage location and security practices must be checked before this notice is published. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free.

Changes and contact

This notice must be updated if the survey questions, tools, research purposes or data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, contact {{PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
