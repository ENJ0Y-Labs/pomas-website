Privacy notice

This notice explains how the POMAS Research Initiative handles information shared through its poultry farmer research survey and this research page. Participation is voluntary.

In short: POMAS uses survey information to understand poultry farm practices and improve its research. The survey is hosted by Google Forms. Google also receives a request when this page loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. Those services have their own privacy terms.

Who is responsible?

The POMAS Research Initiative, a research project based in Nigeria, is responsible for the research use of information submitted to the survey. For privacy requests, contact research@pomas.ng.

What information may be collected?

Depending on the questions you choose to answer, the survey may contain information about your poultry farm, its operations and record-keeping practices, together with contact details you choose to provide for a follow-up conversation. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers, private customer records or other information that is not needed for the research.

Why is it used?

Information is used to understand how poultry farms manage flocks, feed, health, production, inventory, sales, expenses and records. Contact details, if provided, may be used to arrange a research follow-up. POMAS does not sell survey responses or use them to make automated decisions about participants.

Lawful basis

POMAS relies on your voluntary consent to use the information you submit for the research purposes described in this notice. You can choose not to participate. If you provide contact details for a follow-up, those details are used for that requested follow-up. You can withdraw consent by contacting the research team; this does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

Google Forms and Google Fonts

The survey is hosted on Google Forms. When you open and submit the form, Google processes the information and may process technical information under Google's own privacy terms. This page also loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google and Google may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. POMAS does not control Google's independent processing.

Read Google's Privacy Policy for details about Google's services.

Sharing

POMAS does not sell survey responses. Access to responses should be limited to people working on the research. Google processes form data to provide Google Forms. POMAS will not publish information that identifies an individual farmer or farm without an appropriate basis and permission.

How long is information kept?

Identifiable responses are kept while the research is active and for no more than 12 months after the research phase closes. They are then deleted or anonymised unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. Anonymous summaries may be kept for research reporting.

Your choices and rights

You can choose not to take part or skip optional questions. You may contact research@pomas.ng to ask about access to, correction of or deletion of information you submitted, or to object to its use. Some requests may not be possible after information has been irreversibly anonymised. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

POMAS limits access to research responses to people who need them for the stated research. Google applies its own security measures to Google Forms. No internet service can be described as completely risk-free.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated if the survey, research purposes, tools or data practices change. The updated date below will change when a revision is made.
